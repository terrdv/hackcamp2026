## Engraved Earth textures

The 8192 × 4096 color, height and light maps use Natural Earth 1:10m country geometry (public domain).
Source: https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/master/geojson/ne_10m_admin_0_countries.geojson
Terms: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/

Color variation is derived from the user-supplied glass Earth ZIP's original lossless base-color map. The uploaded GLB and Original satellite renderer remain preserved.

The luminous map variation uses the same 8K geographic outlines plus subtle contour-inspired shading derived from the supplied height map; atmospheric rendering is procedural and does not alter geographic coordinates.
